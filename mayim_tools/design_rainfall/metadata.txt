Point of interest may be given by clicking the map canvas, typing coordinates, or selecting a vector point layer (every feature processed). Computes design rainfall depths and 90% confidence bounds from the bundled national grid dataset and exports report-ready tables (CSV / Word) and/or a point layer with results as attributes. Formulae validated against the source WRC report (K5/1060, 2002) - see dev/VALIDATED_FORMULAE.md.
